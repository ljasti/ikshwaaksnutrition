What are Microgreens?

Microgreens are young vegetable greens that are approximately 1-3 inches tall. They are aromatic and packed with nutrients.

Daily Benefits

Rich in potassium, iron, zinc, magnesium, and copper. They are also a great source of beneficial plant compounds like antioxidants.
