How Vegetable Powders Improve Nutrition

April 28, 2026 By Amroth Nutrition Team 3 min read

Eating the recommended daily servings of vegetables is a goal many of us struggle to meet. Between busy work schedules and picky eating habits, nutrition often takes a backseat. This is where Amroth Vegetable Powders come in as a convenient, natural solution.

What are Vegetable Powders?

Our powders are made by dehydrating fresh, locally sourced vegetables at low temperatures. This process removes the water but retains up to 95% of the vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants. The result is a highly concentrated form of nutrition.

Key Benefits

  • Micronutrient Boost: Even a single teaspoon can provide the equivalent of a whole serving of fresh vegetables.
  • Versatility: Easily mix into curries, soups, rotis, or even smoothies without changing the flavor significantly.
  • Long Shelf Life: No more worrying about fresh vegetables wilting in the fridge. Our powders stay fresh for months.

How to Use

The beauty of vegetable powders is their invisibility. If you have kids who avoid greens, simply stir a spoon of our spinach or carrot powder into their dal or paratha dough. It's a nutrition hack that works for the whole family.
