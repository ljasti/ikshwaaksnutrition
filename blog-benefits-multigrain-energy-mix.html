Multigrain powder for everyday use

Amroth Multigrain Powder is a roasted mix intended for cooking with water. Older labels such as Energy Mix or Breakfast Mix refer to the same provisional public requirement.

Preparation stays simple: measure, add water, boil while stirring, season and serve. Pack sizes and exact usage guidance are confirmed when you receive product details.

This article is general kitchen guidance. It is not medical, diagnostic or therapeutic advice.
