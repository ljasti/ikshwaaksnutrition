Benefits of Multigrain Energy Mix

April 28, 2026 By Amroth Nutrition Team 4 min read

Imagine a single drink that combines the nutritional power of over 25 natural ingredients. That is exactly what the Amroth Multigrain Energy Mix offers. Whether you are a busy professional, a growing student, or looking for a healthy alternative for the elderly, this mix is a powerhouse of wellness.

1. Sustained Energy Levels

Unlike sugary energy drinks that give you a "crash" after an hour, our multigrain mix uses complex carbohydrates from Ragi, Jowar, and Bajra. These release energy slowly into your bloodstream, keeping you active for hours.

2. Rich in Plant-Based Protein

With a blend of pulses and nuts like Almonds and Cashews, each serving provides essential amino acids needed for muscle repair and growth. It's an excellent post-workout or breakfast choice.

3. Digestive Health

High fiber content is a hallmark of our ingredients. This supports a healthy gut microbiome and ensures smooth digestion, making it light on the stomach even for those with sensitive digestion.

4. No Preservatives or Chemicals

The biggest benefit is what we don't include. No artificial sweeteners, no chemical stabilizers, and no maltodextrin. Just pure, roasted, and ground natural ingredients.

How to Make the Most of It

For the best results, boil two spoons of the mix in a cup of water for 2-3 minutes. Add a dash of jaggery for sweetness or a pinch of salt for a savory version. It's nutrition, simplified.
