Healthy Breakfast in 2 Minutes

April 28, 2026 By Amroth Nutrition Team 3 min read

We've all heard it: "Breakfast is the most important meal of the day." But in reality, it's often the first thing sacrificed when we're running late. At Amroth, we believe you shouldn't have to choose between your schedule and your health.

The 120-Second Nutrition Plan

Our instant mixes are designed to be ready faster than your coffee. Here is how you can have a complete, nutrient-dense breakfast in just 2 minutes:

  1. The Drink: Mix 2 spoons of Amroth Multigrain Energy Mix with water. Boil for 2 minutes while you get dressed. Pour it into a travel mug. Done.
  2. The Meal: Use our Instant Sambar Mix. Just add hot water and it's ready. Pair it with a quick idli or just have it as a savory soup.

Why it Works

Because our ingredients are pre-roasted and finely ground, they cook almost instantly. You're getting the protein from pulses, fiber from grains, and vitamins from our vegetable-infused blends without the hours of prep work.

No More Excuses

Stop reaching for sugary cereals or skipping meals entirely. With Amroth in your pantry, a healthy start to your day is always just 2 minutes away.
